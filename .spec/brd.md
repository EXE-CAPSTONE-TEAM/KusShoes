# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement
- **Business Problem:** What high-level business pain point is being addressed?
- **Vision & Strategic Value:** Why does this project exist and how does it fit into the broader roadmap?
- **Target Audience / Stakeholders:** Who benefits directly and indirectly (buyers, users, internal ops)?

## 2. Business Objectives & Success Metrics (KPIs)
| Objective | Metric / KPI | Baseline | Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| e.g. Operational Efficiency | Time to complete flow | 15 mins | < 3 mins |
| e.g. Adoption | Active Weekly Users | 0 | 500+ |
| e.g. Reliability | System error rate | N/A | < 0.1% |

## 3. Market & Competitive Context
- **Market Opportunity:** Why solve this now?
- **Competitive Differentiators:** What makes our approach unique vs existing alternatives?
- **Anti-Goals / What We Won't Do:** Business initiatives explicitly excluded to protect focus.

## 4. Financial & Operational Constraints
- **Budget / Cost Ceiling:** Hosting, API credits, LLM token expenditure boundaries.
- **Timeline & Release Target:** Target launch milestone.
- **Compliance & Legal:** Data privacy (GDPR/HIPAA), licensing, security constraints.
